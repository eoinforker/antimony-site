Principles

Three things we will not trade, at any size.

[01]Never alone.

Every product we make sits on the side of the person who made the work, never across the table from them.

[02]Paid when you are.

We earn when the people we serve earn. Orbe is free for creators; brands pay when the work is delivered.

[03]Built to last.

We build for the next forty years, not the next round: plain records, clear rules, nothing we would be embarrassed to explain.
