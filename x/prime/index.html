Company

Antimony is a London company. We build software for the people who make the work, and we sit on their side of the table, never across it. We earn when they earn, and we build for decades rather than rounds: plain records, clear rules, nothing we would be embarrassed to explain.

Antimony, element 51, is almost never found alone in nature; its old name was read as anti-monos, not alone. Alchemists used it to purify gold. Both are what we want our products to do for the people who use them.
