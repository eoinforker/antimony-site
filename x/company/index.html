Company

We sit on the side of the person who made the work, never across the table from them. We earn when they earn, and we build for decades rather than rounds: plain records, clear rules, nothing we would be embarrassed to explain.

Antimony, element 51, is almost never found alone in nature; its old name was read as anti-monos, not alone. Alchemists used it to purify gold. Both are what we want our products to do for the people who use them.
