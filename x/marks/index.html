Five

Antimony holds five of the eight electrons its outer shell has room for. Set the five around a nucleus on a three by three grid and they draw an A. The three empty places, dotted on the large one, are the bonds it makes with others: the element is almost never found alone.

The clearest at 16px, and the truest story

Stibnite

Antimony's ore, stibnite, grows as sprays of long needles from a single seed. Five needles, for the five electrons. Nobody else can claim it, but at a glance it can read as a flame or a lotus.
