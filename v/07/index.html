Dear reader,

Antimony is a small company in London. We make things for people who make things.

Our first product is Orbe. It sits on the creator's side of every sponsorship deal: it prices and negotiates each offer inside the creator's own rules, waits for one tap on anything binding, and holds the brand's money until the work is delivered. It is free for creators; brands pay when the work is delivered.

We will write to you in numbered letters, when there is something worth saying. The first will be Antimony 001.
