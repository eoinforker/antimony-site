Eight marks, drawn by hand.

One is the crystal, redrawn with a brush to see whether the hand alone was what was missing. The other seven are new, from the world of Nurture: things made, things grown, things seen through a window. Each is shown large, reversed, on sky, small, and with the name.

The crystal, by hand

The mark we had, drawn rather than built: six strokes for the hexagon's walls, the A left as paper, the gold coloured in.

The mountain

An A is a mountain. One stroke up and over, the way a child draws Fuji, and the sun coming up behind the peak: what is left at the end of the climb.

The window

A window drawn freehand, a hill line and a low sun inside it. The work is the room; the window is what it is for.

The A, coloured in

A free A drawn in one stroke, and the space inside it coloured in by hand in gold, a little outside the lines.

The road

A road winding up into the hills, as in every print of the Tokaido. Fifty three stations; the work is a long way, walked together.

The sprout

Nurture, literally: a seed that has come up. Two leaves from one stem, a line of earth beneath.

Two

Antimony is almost never found alone. Two strokes standing side by side, one ink, one gold: you and us.

The pencil A

The A drawn with the pencil of an old paint program, one pixel at a time, and its counter filled with the bucket.

These are drawn with a brush I wrote, so each stroke tapers, swells and wanders like ink; it is close to a hand, not a hand. Whichever one you choose, the final mark should be drawn by a person, with a real brush or pencil, and scanned. I can then build everything around that scan.
