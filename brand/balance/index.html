Antimony, the balance

An exact mark, in a world you would want to live in.

Every reference you sent does the same two things. The sign is exact: Arasaka's tree, the triangle in Ghost in the Shell, Berserk's brand, Starset's A. And it is never seen alone, flat, on white. It is struck into metal, burned into skin, printed with grain, painted on a block, lit like a heat camera. That is where the love lives.

Lovable

What this means

We stop asking the mark to be lovable by itself. No mark is: Arasaka's tree on white is a clip art tree. The mark is the exact part and stays exact; the system is a set of worlds it lives in, each with its own material, and every launch, letter and product gets a new one, the way a band gets a new cover.

The mark here is the Crystal A, because your hex picks pointed there. If the exact part is still wrong, these worlds work for any sign: tell me and I will drop another into the same six.
