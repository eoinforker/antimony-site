Timeless Serif

Our typeface for headlines, text and everything said with conviction. It is a variable font, from 200 to 700, with an italic.

Timeless Serif is licensed to Antimony. Ask for the files and the terms of the licence rather than finding them elsewhere. Our wordmarks are drawn from it.

Weights and italic

Headlines at 400, with tight spacing. Text at 500, which holds up better on screen. 600 for the labels in a list or a table. The lighter weights are for very large display and the suffix of a product lockup.

The italic is for names of things and for the odd emphasis. Never set whole paragraphs in it.

The drawn wordmark

The wordmarks are drawn from Timeless Serif, but they are not typed in it. Never set the name in the font and call it the logo: use the files in the kit.

In the lower case wordmark three things are changed by hand. Each comes from the mark: a crystal for the tittle, a join where the stone would be one piece, a cut at the seam's angle.

Commit Mono, for callouts

Commit Mono is for small callouts only: a colour value, a measurement, a file name, a size, the typed line that opens the website. Never for headlines or paragraphs.

Set it small, 10 to 13 pixels, in capitals tracked by a tenth of an em, or in lower case for code. It is open source, under the SIL Open Font License, and in the kit.

Balance against the wordmark

Set the main headline clearly larger or clearly smaller than the wordmark beside it, never the same. The most important thing on the page is the largest; everything else takes less attention.

Left, the headline leads and the capitals sign it. Right, the capitals lead a cover, and the line beneath is quieter.

Use the grid

Everything sits on the document's grid. That does not mean everything is ranged left: it means every block starts and ends on a column, so the page has a reason.

Our pages use twelve columns with a 20 pixel gutter, and a 40 pixel margin on a laptop, 20 on a phone. Labels take three columns; text takes three or six.

Software for the people who make the work.

Every block starts on a column. The headline takes seven, the text takes five, and the two share one column where they meet.

Break the grid, once

Our grid is there to be broken, a little. Step one line of a headline over a few columns, or let one word sit where it is not expected.

Once per layout is enough. A second break is just a mess.
