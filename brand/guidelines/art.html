We draw our own pictures, and we draw them from the mark: its grain, its light, its geometry, its source. Nothing is photographed, borrowed or generated.

Download textures

Textures

Nothing we make is flat. Every ground carries a texture, and every texture is procedural: drawn by code, baked once, and cheap to show.

One texture per layout. Pick it for the ground you need, not for decoration, and keep it quiet enough that the mark or the words lead.

Water light

The light in the crystal, opened out: deep blue below, sky through the middle, pale at the top, with slow caustics and a stippled grain. It is the closest we come to a picture.

Use it full bleed, under the line mark or a single line of type. Never put it inside a frame, and never put a photograph in its place.

Hairline construction

The mark's own construction, drawn as ornament: a long horizontal through its centre, a vertical, the seam run on at sixteen degrees, a few arcs, and one small point of light.

Hairlines only, at about half strength. Use it on a cover, a title slide or the back of something, never behind text, and always with the mark at the crossing.

Rules

No stock photosNot of people, offices, laptops or hands. If a layout seems to need a photograph, it needs fewer things.
No prints, no borrowed artWe do not use other people's pictures, old or new, however fine. Everything here is drawn by us, from the mark.
No generated imagesNo pictures made by image models, and nothing that imitates them.
Light, not glowThe only light is the crystal's: sky, deepening to blue. No neon, no bloom, no gradients for their own sake.
One textureOne texture per layout, one line mark per layout. The page stays nearly empty.
