Antimony, the mark, the prism

The crystal whose tip is an A.

Stibnite, antimony's ore, grows as long prisms with pointed tips: the very shape of a Final Fantasy crystal. Seen front on, the tip of a prism is an A. So the mark is a real crystal, cut like a gem: three faces in proportions of one, two and one, hairline gaps where the facets meet, the light catching one facet. Each is shown large, then lit from inside, sealed, and in fine line, then small and with the name.
