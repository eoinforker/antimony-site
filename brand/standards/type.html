Using type
BalanceSet the main headline either clearly larger or clearly smaller than the wordmark beside it, never the same size. Two things the same size compete.
GridTwelve columns with 24 pixel gutters on screen, 5 mm in print. Type starts on a column.
Break itOnce per layout, and only for a reason: a headline that hangs into the margin, a line that steps over a column. Never twice.
CaseSentence case everywhere, including headlines and buttons. Capitals only for Commit Mono labels, tracked out by 6 to 8 per cent.
FiguresTables and prices in Commit Mono or in tabular figures, so columns line up.
PunctuationBritish spelling. Curly quotation marks. No em or en dashes: a colon, a comma or a full stop does the work, and "to" for ranges.
Other languages

Timeless covers Latin scripts. For others, use Noto Serif and Noto Sans in the matching script, and Source Han Serif and Sans for Chinese, Japanese and Korean, set at the same optical size as the Latin beside them.
