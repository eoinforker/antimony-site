Chapter 01

Logo

A crystal with an A struck through it. It appears everywhere, from a 16 pixel browser tab to the front of a building, so it is drawn to be exact at both.

About the mark

Five readings, each of them true, for anyone who looks for long enough.

Primary lockup

The mark with the wordmark, for everything from the website header to a letterhead. The mark stands 1.42 cap heights tall, centred on the cap height, with 0.42 of a cap height between them.

Stacked

For square and tall spaces. Always centred on the page or the space it sits in.

Symbol

On its own wherever the name is already present or there is no room for it: avatars, app icons, the favicon, stamps.

Wordmark

Timeless Serif at weight 420, kerned as the font intends and tracked in 14 units. The dot of the i is a small triangle: the gold, in cobalt when colour is allowed. Use it alone where the symbol already appears nearby, such as a footer.

Construction
R 50The hexagon's radius, on a 100 unit square. Every point is on it or found from it.
60°The legs run from the top corner to the two lower side corners: an equilateral triangle. Nothing is drawn by eye.
W 9Each strike is 9 units wide, just under a fifth of the radius.
¾The bar is struck three quarters of the way down the A; the counter holds nine sixteenths of the triangle.
4Pieces: two wings, the counter and the foot. Corners are softened by 0.4 units so they print and engrave cleanly.
Clear space and size

Keep the height of the counter, x, clear on every side of the mark and the lockup. Nothing else enters it: not type, not other marks, not the edge of the page.

xThe counter's height: 43.2 units on the 100 unit drawing.
16 pxSmallest symbol on screen, using the drawn 16 pixel version.
96 pxSmallest lockup on screen.
6 mmSmallest symbol in print; 24 mm for the lockup.
Small sizes

Below 48 pixels the mark is not shrunk but drawn: every pixel of the 16 and 32 pixel versions was placed on the grid. These are the files in the favicon.

Our products

Each product carries its own mark, cut from the same kind of metal: one solid block, one strike. Antimony stands beside it as its maker, never inside it.

Working with others

When our name appears with another company's, set the two apart at equal optical size with a hairline and clear space between them, and only where the relationship is real and agreed in writing. Never place a mark so that it implies one company endorses or partners with the other. Never join our mark to anyone else's.

What not to do

Beyond the usual rules for any mark (do not warp it, do not add effects), eight that belong to this one.

As a design element

Two uses beyond the logo itself, and only these two. As a window, the four pieces cut from an archive plate. As a lattice, the mark tiled into Borges's library with one counter lit. Never both on one page, and never a third.
