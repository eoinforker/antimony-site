Logo

A stone split open, with a crystal grown in the break. It has to hold at sixteen pixels and on the front of a building, so it is drawn to be exact at both.

01readings

Four readings, each true, for anyone who looks for long enough.

02lockup

The mark beside the wordmark: the website header, a letterhead, the end of a film. The wordmark stands 0.54 of the mark's height, from the top of the t to the tail of the y, centred on it, with 0.28 of the mark between them.

03symbol

On its own wherever the name is already present or there is no room for it: avatars, app icons, the favicon, the foot of a page.

04wordmark

Drawn from Timeless Serif, in lower case. Three changes make it ours: the tittle of the i is a small crystal, the t is joined to the i, and the tail of the y is cut on the crystal's angle. The tittle is sky on ink and cobalt, and Prussian on paper, where sky is too faint at that size.

05containers

The stone is a tile with corners of 22 units on a 100 unit square. Two other containers exist, each for one job, and the break is the same in all three.

06construction

Every point is found from a handful of numbers on a 100 unit square. Nothing is placed by eye.

100, r 22The tile, and the radius of its corners.
16°The angle of the seam from upright. The crystal is sheared to the same angle, so it grows along the break.
50, 51.5The crystal's centre: 1.5 below the middle, so it sits, rather than floats.
38.4 × 60The crystal: a hexagon 38.4 wide and 60 tall, its shoulders 25.2 from each point.
6.5Clear stone around the crystal at its sides; 7.5 at its points. The cavity is the crystal, grown by that much.
7.5The seam's width beside the crystal. It thins to a fifth of that, 1.5, where it meets the edge.
28%The facet, the crystal's right half, is lit with white at 28 per cent.
07clear space and size

Keep 22 units, the radius of the tile's corner, clear on every side: about a fifth of the mark. Nothing enters it, not type, not other marks, not the edge of the page.

22Units of clear space, measured on the mark, for the symbol and the lockup.
16 pxThe smallest symbol on screen, using the version drawn for small sizes.
120 pxThe narrowest lockup on screen.
6 mmThe smallest symbol in print; 26 mm for the lockup.
08small sizes

Below 30 pixels the mark changes, not just shrinks: the seam widens to 10, the clear stone to 8, and the seam thins less toward the edge, so the break still reads at a glance. These are the files in the favicon.

16, 32Drawn for small sizes.
48 and upThe mark as drawn.
09our products

Each product carries its own mark. Antimony stands beside it as its maker, never inside it.

10working with others

When our name appears beside another company's, set the two at equal optical size, a hairline and clear space between them, and only where the relationship is real and agreed in writing. Never place a mark so that it suggests one company endorses or partners with the other. Never join our mark to anyone else's.

11what not to do

Beyond the usual rules for any mark (do not warp it, do not add effects), eight that belong to this one.

12as a design element

Two uses beyond the logo, and only these two. The window: the crystal holding a picture. The characters: the mark drawn in type on the terminal, as the website opens. Never both in one layout.
