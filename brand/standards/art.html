Chapter 04

Art direction

We do not make pictures of the future. We stand on four hundred years of people making things, and print it again in our own ink.

The archiveThe printThe windowThe ink bloomRulesSources
The archive

Engravings from the Wellcome Collection, all in the public domain, in three families. Matter: crystals, minerals, the old symbols. Method: laboratories, furnaces, the table of what bonds with what. Makers: people at work with their hands.

The print

Every plate is printed as if on a two drum risograph: ink for the engraved line, cobalt for the tone, a hair out of register, with the grain of a real print. Four printings.

The window

The mark's four pieces cut from a plate, the counter left as gold. The one way imagery and the mark meet.

The ink bloom

Plates change by spreading into each other like ink into paper, leaving a thin ink line at the edge. It is two still images and a shader, not a video. Tap it.

Rules
CreditEvery plate carries its credit: title, date if known, and "Wellcome Collection, public domain".
PeopleNever colourise a person, never print a person night side, never let a strike cross a face.
TruthNever make new pictures that imitate the archive and present them as old. If we commission work, it is credited as ours.
OneOne plate per layout. The archive is a source, not a collage.
