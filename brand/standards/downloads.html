01the kit

The kit is sent as one archive, antimony-break-kit.zip. Ask the brand owner for it. Its folders:

logo/The mark, lockup and wordmark on paper, ink and cobalt, and the round mark, as PNG at up to 2400 px.
svg/The mark as SVG: full colour, reversed, one colour, round and small, and the geometry it is drawn from.
icons/Favicons at 16, 32 and 48, and app icons at 180, 192, 512 and 1024.
social/Avatar, LinkedIn cover and banner, X header, link card and three posts.
deck/Title, content and closing slides at 1920 by 1080.
print/Business card front and back, and letterhead, as PDF at true size.
motion/The break, as MP4 at 1920 by 1080 and 1080 square.
