A test of one idea before I redraw anything: that what is missing is the hand. The same marks as before, cut from paper with a torn edge and redrawn eight times a second, the way hand drawn animation boils, on the warm grounds of late afternoon, dusk and first light.

If this is closer to the feeling, the next round is drawn by hand from the start rather than filtered, the palette opens to warm light, and the type and words loosen with it.
