Rain, path traced

What this is
  A frontal view, at eye height, of a pale sandstone garden wall in a green city at blue hour, in gentle rain.
  A large lightbox (1.74 by 2.61 m, half again a six sheet) is set flush into the wall; Antimony's Grow poster
  is drawn live into it in the browser. Ivy falls from the coping. Ferns, pale blue periwinkle, white flowers and
  a little yellow celandine grow in a bed at the wall's foot (only low celandine under the box, so nothing rises
  past its bottom edge), with two low warm path lights. A water rill runs in the paving. On the left there is a
  timber bench and a small tree. On the right is a larch-clad cafe: a steamed window onto a warm room (shelves
  of jars, three pendant lamps, a table, plants), a lit upper window, a canopy with ferns, and a bronze rain chain
  into a stone basin. Beyond the wall are the garden's trees, then planted terraces stepping back into mist.
  Camera: 27 mm on 36 mm, 7.4 m back, 1.55 m high, verticals straight (shift, no tilt), f/4.

  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles on CPU (4 threads), OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, light tree, AgX Base Contrast.
  One cheat, common on film sets: the haze scatters camera rays only (seen, not bounced through).

Run
  blender -b -P rain_scene.py -- --assets <polyhaven dir> --acg <ambientcg dir> --poster <png> \
    --shift 0.03 --cam-x 0 --sky 1.8 --warm-str 1.1 --cafe-w 90 --bollard-w 25 --bollard 40 --poster-str 3 \
    --exposure 0.25 --res 2560x1440 --samples 400 --time 1050 --exr <prefix> --corners corners.json --out beauty.png
  The EXR holds three light groups (sky, poster, warm), each denoised, plus mist, depth and the data passes
  (plant, wet, rill and puddle mask, face, glass).

  blender -b --factory-startup -P rain_compose.py -- --exr <prefix>0001.exr --mode still|spill|loop ...
  Everything is composed in scene-linear light and taken through Blender's own AgX (via OpenColorIO) once.

The loop (6 s, 24 fps, 144 frames, seamless), made from passes, not from per-frame renders
  - rain: three layers of streaks lit by a broad blur of the lit frame, so rain shows where the light is
  - drips: from the canopy edge, the coping and the tree, each gathering then falling on its own period
  - water down the rain chain
  - rings in the puddles and the rill: small radial warps with a faint highlight, flattened by perspective
  - leaves: a small displacement in the wind, weighted by the foliage mask (strongest high up)
  - the warm lights breathe by 5 percent over the loop
  Every motion repeats on a period that divides 6 s, so frame 144 runs straight into frame 1.

Live layer (index.html)
  The render is the background: an AVIF/WebP still, then the H.264 or WebM loop over it. The Grow poster is drawn
  live (WebGL) on a canvas placed exactly over the lightbox face from the exported corners. It keeps Poster 1's
  pointer wind, the water in the crystal and the chip-off into the grass. There is rain beading and running on its
  glass, and rain falling in front. The poster's light on the street is a precomputed pass: (all lights) minus
  (all but the poster), in display terms. It sits over the video in difference blend, and its opacity follows the
  poster's measured brightness, so the street is lit by what the poster shows (and dark before it comes on).

Honest limits
  - The poster image seen in puddle reflections and glass is the rest state; when shards chip off, the
    reflections do not change.
  - Drips and rain do not cast light or wet anything new; leaves sway by image warp, not by moving geometry.
  - The terraces beyond are procedural boxes with a window shader and fern scatter; they read in the mist but
    would not hold up close.
  - Haze does not drift.
