Rain: assets used in the render (all CC0, no attribution required; listed anyway)

Poly Haven (polyhaven.com)
  beach_parking (HDRI, 4k): the blue dusk sky light, cooled
  wood_planks_grey (texture, 2k): the cafe's larch cladding
  fern_02, shrub_04, shrub_01, periwinkle_plant (petals recoloured pale blue), celandine_01, moss_01 (models, 2k)
  tree_small_02 (model, 2k): the tree, the garden trees, and its leaf sprigs for the ivy
  modular_street_seating (model, 2k): the bench
  potted_plant_01, potted_plant_02 (models, 2k): in the cafe

ambientCG (ambientcg.com)
  Bricks084 (2K): the pale sandstone wall, its coping, the kerb, the sill and the basin
  PavingStones128 (2K): the paving, with water held in its joints from the height map

Everything else (the lightbox, the rain chain, the rill, the room, the terraces beyond, the rain, drips and rings)
is built in rain_scene.py and rain_compose.py. The poster is Antimony's own Grow page, drawn live in the browser.
