Concept Nier, path traced

What this is
  A quiet city long abandoned and gently reclaimed, in bright, soft, slightly washed daylight. Seven concrete
  towers stand in the haze, two leaning, four with their tops sheared off; whole bays are punched out of their
  walls with rebar left hanging, and ivy climbs from their feet and pours from their broken tops, heaviest on the
  near tower. Trees grow on the breaks. A wide meadow of tall grass, yellow celandine and empodium, a little pale
  blue heliophila and white dandelion runs where the streets were. In the foreground the Break stands clean in
  the grass: a dark honed stone, its cut faces darker still, the crystal as real glass held to sky #94E6FF.
  The feeling of NieR: Automata's city ruins; none of its assets, characters, logos or text.
  Camera: 40 mm on 36 mm, 1.45 m high, pitched up 4 degrees, f/4 focused on the stone 14.8 m away.

  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles on CPU (4 threads), OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, light tree, AgX.
  One cheat, as in Rain: the haze scatters camera rays only (seen, not bounced through), so the low sun still
  shapes it through the towers without volume speckle.

Keeping the mark whole
  Nothing grows on the stone (its footprint is kept clear), and nothing in front of it may rise over it: every
  plant between the lens and the stone has its height capped at the line from the lens to the foot of the stone's
  front face (each view ray is met with the face itself). The loop never warps the stone or the crystal and no
  seed crosses them. On the page, the crop anchors on the stone's centre across and its foot down, so the whole
  mark stays in view at any window shape; phones get their own 9:16 crop centred on it.

Run
  blender -b -P nier_meadow.py -- --ph <assets/polyhaven> --acg <assets/ambientcg> \
    --res 2560x1440 --samples 48 --time 1150 --exr <prefix_>
  It writes <prefix_>0001.exr (denoised beauty, depth, and masks for plants, the stone and the crystal) and
  <prefix_>mark.json (where the stone lands on the frame).

  blender -b --factory-startup -P nier_compose.py -- --exr <prefix_>0001.exr --mark <prefix_>mark.json \
    --mode still|loop --out <png or frames/f_%04d.png> [--phone <png or %04d>] [--res 1920] \
    --exposure 0.3 --sat 1.08 --lift 0.8
  Composed in scene-linear light, through Blender's own AgX (Base Contrast) once, then a gentle display grade:
  pale pastel blacks, warm highlights, slight lateral colour, vignette, fine grain.

The loop (6 s, 24 fps, 144 frames, seamless), made from passes, not per-frame renders
  - wind in the grass: a gust that travels left to right, weighted by the plant mask; nearer blades move further
  - the haze breathes: two soft noise fields drift through one period each, only in the distance
  - seeds in the warm air: dandelion fluff turning on closed paths, drifting in and out of the light; a few near
    ones are soft discs, out of focus
  Every motion repeats on a period that divides 6 s, so frame 144 runs straight into frame 1.

The page (index.html)
  The render is the background: an AVIF/WebP still, then the WebM or H.264 loop over it (phones load the 9:16
  crop). The opening is short and skippable (click, key, scroll or touch; once per session): the haze over the
  meadow thins and clears, then the name arrives. The wordmark is the serif capitals with the crystal cut A, the
  cut filled with sky, over "Software, made on purpose." Orbe, immiscible and Idyll are three quiet text links.
  Sound is built in the browser (wind through tall grass from filtered pink noise, a warm distant air of three
  quiet tones), muted until the one small toggle is pressed. Reduced motion shows the still with no opening.

Honest limits
  - The sun is soft: the light reads as bright hazy daylight, not hard sun; shadows across the meadow are gentle.
  - The towers are bevelled slabs dressed with a scanned facade and concrete; their window grid is regular, and at
    this distance the rebar is barely visible.
  - The ivy is scanned leaf cards scaled with distance so it reads through the haze; close up it would need real
    vines.
  - The grass moves by image warp, not by moving geometry.

The paper, the beauty pass and the breathing layer (October 27)
  The paper: a tri-fold pamphlet, sun-faded and torn at one edge (a corner gone, a small tear at the top), Z-folded
  and caught upright in the grass, leaning back, a little left of and in front of the stone so it sits in the phone
  crop too. Real geometry in nier_meadow.py --paper <rgba print png> (the torn edge is the print's alpha; ambientCG
  Paper002 grain; thin paper partly translucent so the low sun comes through it). It is tagged 0.35 in the plant
  mask, so it stirs a little with the grass in the loop. Grass between the lens and the pamphlet is kept below the
  line to its foot (as for the stone) so it is seen. Its outline is written into <prefix>mark.json for the page.
  The letter: as the other pages, lit as the meadow (warm sun from above, a green bounce from the grass), the sheet
  sun-bleached along one side, Z-folded, with its corner torn away and a fibrous foot.
  Beauty pass: a low warm sun (8 degrees up, from the front left) so the grass and flowers are backlit and
  translucent and shadows run long towards the lens; a warmer, less grey sky; warm luminous haze between the
  towers; grass and petals given a translucent component; the stone smaller (1.6 m) and now the shared stone
  (directions/stone/stone.py): scanned basalt, softened, chipped edges, lichen and moss at the foot, sky glass;
  low turf may rise a few centimetres at its face, so it stands in the grass instead of on a bare strip.
  Compose: blacks lifted warm, not blue (--lift-col), warmer highlights (--warm).
  Breathing: the loop drawn through its depth (assets/nier-depth.png, pivoted and pinned on the stone), a slow drift
  and push over about a minute, pointer and tilt parallax, slow cloud shadows sliding across the meadow, a light
  that swells and settles, now and then a few far birds crossing between the towers. Sound: a gust through the
  grass that answers the pointer, a skylark high and far off now and then, a soft call when the birds cross, and
  everything goes soft while the letter is open.
  Final render: 2560x1440, 48 samples (time cap 1100 s, reached the samples first): 777 s of rendering.
  nier_meadow.py --ph <polyhaven> --acg <ambientcg> --paper <pamphlet rgba png> --s 0.016 --thick 0.42 --foot-h 0.07
    --sun-el 8 --sun-az 135 --sun 15 --sky 0.6 --haze-g 0.72 --pam-s 1.35 --sun-col 1.0,0.76,0.52 --sky-wash 0.3
    --wash-col 1.06,0.96,0.84 --haze 0.0005 --haze-col 1.0,0.9,0.78 --tl 0.35 --res 2560x1440 --samples 48 --time 1100
  nier_compose.py ... --exposure 0.3 --sat 1.1 --lift 0.7 --lift-col 0.075,0.066,0.05 --warm 1.6
  The page's low mist band is thinner (11vh, 6vh on phones) so it does not wash over the pamphlet.
  Honest limits, new: the sun is low and behind the towers' side, but the long shadows across the meadow are still
  faint in the haze; the light reads as warm and backlit rather than as hard golden sun. The pamphlet is small and
  reads as a pale, crumpled sheet rather than as a printed pamphlet until it is opened.
