# Assets for the Unreal world

All from the owner's Fab library, downloaded on 27 Oct 2026. The files live only on the GPU box, in
`/workspace/fab/src/<name>/`, and are **never committed or published**. The repo is public, and Fab's licences allow
using the assets in our renders but not redistributing the source files.

Licence column: **Megascans** items (with a Quixel `.json`) are under the Fab Standard License. The community
listings need their licence read off their Fab page (some are CC BY and need a credit line); until then they say
"check".
