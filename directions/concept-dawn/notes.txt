Dawn over the Plate, path traced

What this is
  Sunrise over a field of flowers. The camera stands 1 m above the flowers on a low crest. Beyond a dry moat
  (a pale concrete lip with mist in it) the old world stands quiet: a broken plate on its pillars, two cold
  stacks, far ruins in the haze. The sun sits low under the plate's broken end, so the haze carries shafts
  between the pillars. In the field the Break stands on a low rise, square to the camera so the seam reads
  top to bottom and the sky crystal is lit from behind.
  Camera: 24 mm on 36 mm, eye 1.0 m, pitch 3.5 degrees, yaw 10 degrees left, f/4 focused on the stone (17 m).
  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles CPU, OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, AgX Base Contrast, bloom.
  One cheat: the haze is lit by the sun only (world scatter off), so light has shape instead of a grey veil.

Run
  blender -b -P dawn_scene.py -- --assets <dir with pink_sunrise_8k.exr, tex/, grass_medium_01/> \
    --res 2560x1440 --samples 384 --time 1140 --out beauty.png --passes passes.png
  python3 dawn_compose.py --beauty beauty.png --passes passes.png --out assets --shots shots --crf 19 --vp9-crf 30
  Final render: 1178 s (about 20 minutes) on 4 shared CPU threads.
  passes.png: R plants, G the mark, B depth / 400 m.

The loop (6 s, 24 fps, 144 frames, seamless), from one render and its passes
  - flowers and grass sway by a small warp weighted by the plant pass, stronger the nearer they are
  - the far air breathes: a soft field that drifts exactly one tile per loop
  - a few motes drift on closed paths, brighter on the sun's side
  - the mark is masked out of every motion and never moves
  All periods divide 6 s, so frame 144 runs into frame 1. Halation, vignette and grain are laid over each frame.

Page
  index.html: the AVIF/WebP still, the loop over it (WebM or MP4; phone crop on portrait screens, centred on the
  stone), a short opening (the field comes up out of the dark, then the type), once a session and skippable,
  the wordmark with "Software, made on purpose.", Orbe, immiscible and Idyll as quiet links, and a sound toggle
  (muted by default): morning wind, a faint warm chord and a few far birds, all synthesised.
  Reduced motion: no loop and no opening, the still only. ?t=<ms> freezes the loop for screenshots; ?again replays the opening.
  The mark is whole at rest at 1440x900 and 390x844; narrower landscape screens crop around it (object-position at its x).

Honest limits
  - The field between the flowers and the moat reads as a pale grey-lilac band, not as flowers; the far field is
    a procedural colour texture under haze, and it is the weakest part of the frame.
  - The old structures are simple masses; they read as silhouettes in haze but not as detailed ruins.
  - The flowers are one scanned cushion (heliophila) tinted three ways plus scanned empodium; up close they are
    real, at distance they are a speckle.
  - The phone loop is a 608x1080 crop of the 1920x1080 loop, so it is softer than the phone still.
  - Haze does not truly drift; the sway is an image warp, not moving geometry.

The paper, the beauty pass and the breathing layer (October 27)
  The paper: a folded note (18 by 25 cm, folded once), held on a low mossy rock about 3.8 m from the lens by a pebble,
  its upper leaf opened by the morning air so the low sun shines through it. Real geometry in dawn_scene.py --paper
  <print png> (Poly Haven rock_moss_set_01 for the rock and pebble, ambientCG Paper002 for the paper's grain;
  thin paper is a third translucent). It sits low and left of the stone, on the line towards it, so it is in the
  phone crop too. The flowers along the line from the lens to it are kept short so it is seen over them.
  dawn_scene.py --paper-json writes its outline for the page's button. In the mark pass the note is tagged 0.45 and
  the rock 0.3, so the loop never sways them as plants; the note's lifted leaf lifts and settles a little on its own.
  The letter: as Rain's, lit as the field is (sunrise warm low from the left, the shade a cool rose), with the fold.
  Beauty pass: the far field is flowers all the way to the moat and on up the rise beyond it (two more scatters of
  larger cushions, so they read through the haze as drifts), a blush tint added to the scanned heliophila for one
  pink and gold palette, less general haze, stronger shafts between the pillars, a brighter sun. The stone is now
  the shared stone (directions/stone/stone.py): scanned basalt, softened and chipped arrises, dew and moss at its
  foot, true sky glass. Compose: depth is decoded from the passes' sRGB curve (it was read as encoded before), and a
  gentle split tone (rose shadows, gold light).
  Fixed: Collection Info picks instances in name order, so flower colours are now indexed by name.
  Breathing: the loop drawn through its depth (assets/dawn-depth.png, pivoted on the stone, which is pinned so it
  never moves), a slow drift and a push over a minute, pointer and tilt parallax, slow cloud shade sliding over the
  land, a light that swells and settles, now and then a few far birds crossing the sky (with a soft call when the
  sound is on). Sound: the wind gusts through the flowers as the pointer moves, a very low far hum of the old
  structures, and everything goes soft while the letter is open.
  Final render: 2560x1440, 384 samples capped at 1080 s: 1105 s of rendering. (A first final at 1111 s was
  discarded: the stronger shafts showed the volume's box edges as hard lines in the sky; the shafts volume now
  fades to nothing towards its faces, --shaft-soft.)
  dawn_scene.py --assets <dl> --paper <note png> --paper-json <json> --far-flowers 70000 --beyond-flowers 40000
    --haze 0.00008 --shafts 0.0015 --sun 10 --note-open 140 --rock-s 0.32 --rock-sz 0.33 --res 2560x1440
    --samples 384 --time 1080 --out beauty.png --passes passes.png
  dawn_compose.py ... --crf 21 --vp9-crf 32 --tone 1.0
  Honest limits, new: the note is lit from behind and its face is in shade, so it reads as a pale card rather than a
  glowing one; the rock under it is a dark mass. The far field reads as drifts of colour, still not as flowers.
