Dawn over the Plate, path traced

What this is
  Sunrise over a field of flowers. The camera stands 1 m above the flowers on a low crest. Beyond a dry moat
  (a pale concrete lip with mist in it) the old world stands quiet: a broken plate on its pillars, two cold
  stacks, far ruins in the haze. The sun sits low under the plate's broken end, so the haze carries shafts
  between the pillars. In the field the Break stands on a low rise, square to the camera so the seam reads
  top to bottom and the sky crystal is lit from behind.
  Camera: 24 mm on 36 mm, eye 1.0 m, pitch 3.5 degrees, yaw 10 degrees left, f/4 focused on the stone (17 m).
  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles CPU, OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, AgX Base Contrast, bloom.
  One cheat: the haze is lit by the sun only (world scatter off), so light has shape instead of a grey veil.

Run
  blender -b -P dawn_scene.py -- --assets <dir with pink_sunrise_8k.exr, tex/, grass_medium_01/> \
    --res 2560x1440 --samples 384 --time 1140 --out beauty.png --passes passes.png
  python3 dawn_compose.py --beauty beauty.png --passes passes.png --out assets --shots shots --crf 19 --vp9-crf 30
  Final render: 1178 s (about 20 minutes) on 4 shared CPU threads.
  passes.png: R plants, G the mark, B depth / 400 m.

The loop (6 s, 24 fps, 144 frames, seamless), from one render and its passes
  - flowers and grass sway by a small warp weighted by the plant pass, stronger the nearer they are
  - the far air breathes: a soft field that drifts exactly one tile per loop
  - a few motes drift on closed paths, brighter on the sun's side
  - the mark is masked out of every motion and never moves
  All periods divide 6 s, so frame 144 runs into frame 1. Halation, vignette and grain are laid over each frame.

Page
  index.html: the AVIF/WebP still, the loop over it (WebM or MP4; phone crop on portrait screens, centred on the
  stone), a short opening (the field comes up out of the dark, then the type), once a session and skippable,
  the wordmark with "Software, made on purpose.", Orbe, immiscible and Idyll as quiet links, and a sound toggle
  (muted by default): morning wind, a faint warm chord and a few far birds, all synthesised.
  Reduced motion: no loop and no opening, the still only. ?t=<ms> freezes the loop for screenshots; ?again replays the opening.
  The mark is whole at rest at 1440x900 and 390x844; narrower landscape screens crop around it (object-position at its x).

Honest limits
  - The field between the flowers and the moat reads as a pale grey-lilac band, not as flowers; the far field is
    a procedural colour texture under haze, and it is the weakest part of the frame.
  - The old structures are simple masses; they read as silhouettes in haze but not as detailed ruins.
  - The flowers are one scanned cushion (heliophila) tinted three ways plus scanned empodium; up close they are
    real, at distance they are a speckle.
  - The phone loop is a 608x1080 crop of the 1920x1080 loop, so it is softer than the phone still.
  - Haze does not truly drift; the sway is an image warp, not moving geometry.
