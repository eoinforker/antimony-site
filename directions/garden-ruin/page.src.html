Antimony

Inside a vast old stone hall, its roof broken open, one shaft of warm daylight falls through drifting dust onto a bed of white, pale blue and yellow flowers growing through the cracked floor. Among them stands the Antimony stone, split by its seam, its sky blue crystal lit by the sun. Still water in the floor holds the light. Through a great open arch at the far end, a second roofless hall stands in sunlit haze with low ridges beyond. Ivy climbs the columns, and a folded letter leans against a fallen block at the edge of the light.
