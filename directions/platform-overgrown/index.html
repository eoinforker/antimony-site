Antimony

The same underground platform years later, grown green: daylight falls through an opening in the tiled vault, ferns and long grass fill the tracks, ivy hangs from the cornice and shallow clear water lies on the platform. Across the tracks a long lightbox still shows a daylight meadow, the Antimony mark and the line: software for the people who make the work. A figure stands looking at it. Now and then a small glass roofed tram glides through.
