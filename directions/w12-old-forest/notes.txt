The Old Forest, path traced

What this is
  Morning in a towering old forest. Cream mist hangs between the trunks; several distinct shafts of sun
  slant down from the upper left through gaps in the canopy and catch golden crowns; one great trunk
  (Poly Haven jacaranda_tree scaled to an ancient giant) stands at the right on its roots. The Antimony
  stone (the shared stone module, exact Break geometry, mossy at the foot) stands among scanned roots in a
  clear pool of light, turned square to the camera so the seam reads top to bottom. A letter lies tucked
  at its right foot in the light.
  Camera: 26 mm on 36 mm, 1.55 m high, about 9.2 m from the stone, f/4.
  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles on CPU, OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, light tree, AgX.

Cheats, as on a film set
  - The canopy is a flag: an opaque sheet 34 m up with real holes cut where shafts should fall, each chosen
    so its shaft crosses the view, and one over the stone. The trees cast no shadow of their own, so only
    the flag shapes the light and the shafts stay clean and separate.
  - The fill is a soft cream world light (the mist's own glow); no photographed sky shows through.
  - The far trunks fade into cream by a depth haze in the compositor as well as the volume.

Run (S is the scratchpad)
  flock S/blender.lock nice -n 5 blender -b -P old_forest.py -- --res 2560x1440 --samples 256 --tl 930 \
      --work S/w11-13/fin12 --out S/w11-13/fin12/graded.png
  python3 old_forest_compose.py --work S/w11-13/fin12 --out .
  Data pass: R disparity 2/d, G ferns 1 and leaves 0.5, B stone 0.5, crystal 1, letter 0.75.
  Final render: 2560x1440, 256 samples capped at 930 s: 956 s of rendering.

The loop (8 s, 24 fps, 192 frames, seamless), made from the still and its data pass
  - the light shifts: the shafts swell and ebb, a slow change travelling along them
  - motes of dust turn slowly, seen only where the light is
  - golden leaves fall slowly, turning, each crossing the frame once a loop, hidden behind nearer things
  - the ferns stir a little; the stone, crystal and letter hold perfectly still
  H.264 0.9 MB, VP9 0.35 MB, 1920x1080. Still: AVIF/WebP; phone still: a 9:16 crop centred on the stone.

The page (index.html): as the Blue Field's (see ../w11-blue-field/notes.txt), with this world's paper and sound.
  The paper: "Read the note" over the letter at the stone's foot; it lifts into a warm cream sheet lit from
  the upper left, as the shafts fall. Sound (muted until the toggle): morning air in high leaves that rises
  with the pointer, a low warm chord (D, A, D, F sharp) breathing, and now and then a bird: a few short
  falling whistled notes, off to one side. Checked with Playwright at 1440x900 and 390x844: no console
  errors, no overflow, the note opens and closes.

Honest limits
  - The shafts all come from one side, as a low sun gives; they are clear in the upper left and fainter
    across the right, where the great tree stands in shade.
  - A few pale gaps remain between far trunks at the horizon (mist, not sky), softer than before.
  - The letter is small in the frame and sits at the stone's foot beside the roots rather than under them.
  - Leaves and motes are composited, so they do not cast shadows or catch the shafts' exact edges.
