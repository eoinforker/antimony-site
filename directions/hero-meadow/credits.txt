Hero Meadow: assets and licences

All third-party assets are from Poly Haven (polyhaven.com) and ambientCG (ambientcg.com),
and are CC0 (public domain).
Nothing else is photographic. The stone's form, the crystal, the tall grass blades, the seed
heads and the wildflowers (buttercups, a pale blue five-petal flower, daisies) are built in
meadow_scene.py.

HDRI (lighting and sky)
  drackenstein_quarry, 4k HDR          https://polyhaven.com/a/drackenstein_quarry

Textures
  ambientCG Rock041, 2K (colour, normal, roughness): the stone, darkened and honed,
  blended by noise with
  Poly Haven seaside_rock, 2k (diffuse, normal, roughness)
                                       https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Rock041
                                       https://polyhaven.com/a/seaside_rock
  ambientCG Ground037, 2K, blended with Poly Haven sparse_grass, 2k: the ground under the grass
                                       https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Ground037
                                       https://polyhaven.com/a/sparse_grass

Models
  grass_medium_01, 2k (LOD1 clumps): the lower layer of the meadow; its scanned atlas also
  colours the procedural tall blades and supplies the seed-head cards
                                       https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_medium_01

The Break mark geometry is Antimony's own (break-geometry.json).
