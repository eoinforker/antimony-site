Hero Meadow, path traced

What this is
  The Antimony stone (the shared module, directions/stone/stone.py: exact Break geometry, honed
  basalt, softened and chipped arrises, moss at the foot, the crystal as sky glass) standing on a low
  rise in a summer meadow in the low, warm light of late afternoon. Beyond it the field falls into a
  shallow dip and rises to a far ridge with a hedgerow of scanned trees and shrubs; soft hills lie
  kilometres off, and above is a wide sky of sculpted cumulus.
  Camera: 45 mm on 36 mm, 1.9 m high, 12 m from the stone, pitched up 3.5 degrees, f/2.2,
  focused on the stone. Sun about 19 degrees left of the view and 4.6 degrees up.
  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles on CPU (4 threads), OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, light tree,
  AgX, with a soft bloom in the compositor.

Assets: see credits.txt (Poly Haven and ambientCG, all CC0).

Light
  The puresky HDRI lights the scene (4k), and camera rays see its 8k version, so the clouds stay
  sharp. Its sun is softened by haze, so a matched sun lamp (warm, 0.6 degrees) at the HDRI's sun
  direction gives the raking light and long shadows. Refractive caustics from that sun go through
  the crystal (MNEE, set up by the stone module).

The paper
  A folded letter is tucked into the seam near the top of the stone, its free edge lifting. It is
  a real object in the render, with the same camera, light and shadow. Its frame position is
  exported (<out>.paper.json) and placed over the picture as a focusable button ("Read the note").
  Opening it eases the view in towards the paper; the note then lifts and settles flat, showing
  the approved words on ambientCG Paper002, lit to match the scene. Escape, the close mark or a
  click outside eases back. After a quiet spell the paper catches the light twice.
  Other interaction: a few pixels of pointer parallax; with sound on, the wind swells as the
  pointer moves and when the note is opened.

Grade windows (meadow_post.py), as a colourist would
  - The crystal's colour is pulled onto #94E6FF, keeping its light.
  - The grey undersides of the clouds are warmed (peach, never amber).
  - Aerial perspective: distance takes on the colour of the sky just above it, from the depth pass.
  Then lens distortion, lateral colour, halation, a vignette and grain.

Run (S is the scratchpad; see hm/work/common.sh for the full argument list)
  blender -b -P meadow_scene.py -- --assets <grass, rock> --acg <assets/ambientcg> --ph <assets/polyhaven>
    --stone directions/stone/stone.py --hdri <kloppenheim_06_puresky_4k.hdr> --sky <..._8k.hdr>
    --sun-u 0.612 --sun-el 4.6 --sun-str 3 --sun-az 19 --lens 45 --pitch 3.5 --cam-h 1.9 --mound 0.75
    --my 12 --thick 0.45 --yaw -9 --res 2560x1600 --samples 512 --time 1140 --out day.png --passes day_data.png
  For the phone framing, add --crop phone --res 1080x2340.
  python3 meadow_post.py still day.png hero-desktop.png day_data.png --crystal 0.6
  python3 meadow_post.py loop day.png day_data.png <frames> --w 1920 --secs 8 --crystal 0.6 --grain 0.006
  then ffmpeg: H.264 CRF 19 to 21, VP9 CRF 28 to 30.

The loop (8 s, 24 fps, 192 frames, seamless), built from the one still and its data pass
  The data pass is a 16-bit PNG: R log depth (ln(1 + d) / ln(4001)), G blade height fraction,
  B mask (crystal 1, stone and paper 0.75, grass and flowers 0.5, sky 0).
  - Grass sway: a displacement field from three travelling waves, scaled by blade height (tips
    move most) and by nearness; the stone, crystal and paper are held still.
  - Light breathing: about 3 percent, more in the highlights.
  - Warm pollen on closed drifting paths.
  Every motion repeats a whole number of times in 8 s, so the last frame runs into the first.

Honest limits
  - The meadow flowers are modelled in the script; Poly Haven's scanned flowers are dry-country
    species that would not belong here.
  - The sway is a warp of the still, not a re-render. The clouds do not move, and the far hedgerow
    does not stir.
  - The hedgerow trees are small scanned trees scaled up; at 300 m and in haze they read well,
    closer they would not.

Bloom (the night variant, night-desktop.png and night-phone.png) is shelved; its page and loops
were removed.
