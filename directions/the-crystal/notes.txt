The Crystal, path traced

What this is
  Golden hour over a vast valley of fir forest, filled with mist. On a far hill, about 1.6 km off,
  stands the Antimony stone at the scale of a landmark: the shared stone module's monolith (exact
  Break geometry: the tile with corners of radius 22 on 100, the seam at 16 degrees cut through, the
  sky crystal set in its cavity), 300 m square, of scanned basalt with softened, chipped arrises and
  moss at its foot. The low sun stands behind a ridge of firs to the left; a broken cloud deck,
  seen only as shadow, cuts its light into shafts through the haze. A small ruined gatehouse and two
  broken towers stand far off in the mist. In the foreground, the grass of the ridge's lip, and in it
  a weathered letter held by a small stone.
  Camera: 35 mm on 36 mm, on the ridge 3 m above the grass, about 23 m back from the lip, tilted up
  3.5 degrees, f/16.

  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles on CPU (4 threads), OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, AgX Medium High
  Contrast. Sky: Poly Haven kloppenheim_06_puresky, held under a golden gradient with a broad glow
  about the sun. Scans: Poly Haven fir_tree_01 (its lightest LOD only), island_tree_01,
  grass_medium_02, rock_moss_set_01; ambientCG Grass004, Rock030, Rock041, Paper002 (and the
  stone module's own Rock035, Rock041, Moss002). See credits.txt.

How the crystal is held to sky blue
  The stone module's sky glass, plus an object-masked grade in the compositor (object index pass)
  that holds the crystal's pixels on #94E6FF before the bloom; its inner light reaches the stone and
  the land but never the mist (light linking), so it cannot veil itself.

Run (always under the shared lock)
  blender -b -P the_crystal.py -- --out beauty.png --masks masks.png --w 2560 --h 1440 --samples 200 --tl 1150
    writes the still, a flat mask render from the same camera (R the crystal, G the near grass,
    B distance) and beauty.json (where the stone, the crystal and the letter land, in pixels).
  blender -b -P the_crystal.py -- --out letterinfo.png --letter-pass letter.png --w 2560 --h 1440 --samples 200
    the letter alone over a transparent film, the grass in front holding it out (about 15 s).
  python3 crystal_compose.py --still beauty.png --masks masks.png --letter letter.png --info letterinfo.json --out assets --shots shots
    lays the letter in, grades the still (gold against dark, the crystal kept blue, a soft
    vignette), writes the stills (PNG, WebP, AVIF, wide and phone) and composes the loop.

The loop (6 s, 24 fps, 144 frames, seamless), composed from the one still and its masks
  - mist drifts through the valley: two soft layers sway along it at different speeds, weighted to
    the middle distance and kept off the stone and the lip
  - the light breathes: the sun's side of the sky swells and settles, and the crystal glows and settles
  - seven birds wheel beside the stone, far off; their size gives the stone its scale
  - the grass on the lip sways
  - fine grain, new on every frame
  Every motion divides 144 frames, so frame 144 runs straight into frame 1.

Page (index.html)
  The still (AVIF, WebP), then the loop over it (WebM or H.264; a tall cut centred on the stone on
  phones). The camera breathes: a very slow push and settle, and a little parallax with the pointer.
  A short opening, once a session: the frame rises out of ink while the mist lifts off the valley,
  then the type arrives; a touch, key or scroll skips it. The wordmark and "Software, made on
  purpose." bottom left; one sound toggle top right; nothing else. The letter in the grass is a
  button ("Read the note"): the view eases towards it and the sheet lifts and lies flat as the
  letter, with the approved words; Escape, the close mark or a click outside eases back. After a
  while without input the letter catches the light, once in a while. Sound is made in the browser
  and silent until asked: wind over the ridge in slow gusts (it swells as the pointer moves), far
  birds over the valley, and a low warm tone. Reduced motion: no opening, no loop, no parallax, the
  letter crossfades.

Honest limits
  - The loop is made from one still: the mist and grass move by image layers and warps, the birds
    are drawn, and the light breathes as a grade; nothing is re-rendered per frame.
  - The letter is its own object pass, coloured to the evening light in the composer; up close it
    reads a little clean against the grass.
  - The stone's foot meets the hill just behind the crest line, so its moss shows only faintly.
