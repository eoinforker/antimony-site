Antimony

Golden hour over a vast valley of fir forest and old ruins, filled with mist. On a far hill stands the Antimony mark as an ancient monolith, a dark stone split by a seam with a sky blue crystal in its heart, the height of a mountain. Birds wheel beside it. On the grassy ridge in the foreground a weathered letter lies pinned under a small stone.

Software, made on purpose.
