The Blue Field, path traced

What this is
  Night after rain. A vast field of scanned flowers whose petals are tinted a true sky blue to white-blue,
  translucent and faintly luminous, larger near the camera and soft at the lens. Under a moonlit cloud deck
  (the Poly Haven scan at 4k, cooled toward cobalt, faint stars only where the cloud is thin), needles of rock
  stand far off in banks of mist: Poly Haven sea cliff scans stood on end and tapered in Python, so their
  surfaces are real scan. Behind them, ranges of hills fade into the mist. The Antimony stone (the shared stone
  module, exact Break geometry) stands wet and clean in the field, with no growth on it: honed grey basalt with
  faint carving, square to the camera so the seam reads top to bottom. Its crystal glows gently, with depth
  inside (faint internal planes, thick parts leaning toward cobalt) and a fine glint of the moon along one
  arris. At its foot, on dark wet turf, a letter lies folded once and half open in the crystal's light.
  Two renders: the wide frame (32 mm on 36 mm, 1.45 m high, 10.7 m from the stone, f/2.8, focused on it) and a
  tall frame composed for phones from the same place, turned onto the stone and looking a little higher.
  Blender 4.5.15 LTS, Cycles on CPU, OpenImageDenoise, adaptive sampling, light tree, AgX.

Light, and the cheats, as on a film set
  - The moon lamp stands to the right, a little toward the camera, so it rakes the stone's face. The bright
    moonlit cloud in the sky lies further round, upper right; a rim light from that direction lights the
    needles alone (light linking), so they read as sculpted rock against the mist.
  - Two broad soft lights stand in for the sky's light on the stone, one above left and one low left; they
    light the stone alone, so the field keeps its own light.
  - The crystal's glint: a small source on the mirror direction of one polished arris, seen by the crystal
    alone and shadowed by nothing else, so it draws a fine line along the edge.
  - A point light in front of the crystal stands in for its glow on the flowers and the letter; a soft light
    behind the stone lights the flowers there so the seam and the outline read; up to 18 small hidden lights among
    the near flowers let their glow pool on the grass.
  - Mist: a low blanket, banks at the needles' middle heights, more around their feet, thinning beyond about
    1.5 km so the far ranges stand as a soft skyline. The camera sees to 8 km.
  - Nothing is scattered where it would stand in front of the mark or the letter. Each plant is tested in the
    camera's view, counting its spread and the lens's blur at its distance, so no soft edge reaches the mark.
  - The crystal's colour is pulled onto #94E6FF in the compose, keeping its light and shade.

Run (S is the scratchpad; always under the shared lock)
  flock S/blender.lock nice -n 5 blender -b -P blue_field.py -- --res 2560x1440 --samples 256 --tl 3300 --vstep 6 \
      --data 1 --work S/w11p/finW --out S/w11p/finW/graded.png
  flock S/blender.lock nice -n 5 blender -b -P blue_field.py -- --phone 1 --res 1080x2340 --samples 224 --tl 2700 \
      --vstep 6 --data 1 --work S/w11p/finP --out S/w11p/finP/graded.png
  python3 blue_field_compose.py --work S/w11p/finW --work-phone S/w11p/finP --out .
  Each render writes raw.png, base.png (graded, no grain), data.png (16 bit: R disparity 2/d, G plants,
  B stone 0.5, crystal 1, paper 0.75) and frame.json.
  Final renders: wide 2560x1440, all 256 samples, 1754 s; tall 1080x2340, all 224 samples, 1719 s.
  The compose (both sets, stills and loops) takes about 8 minutes.
  The first version of the scene is kept as blue_field_v1.py and its compose as blue_field_compose_v1.py.

The loops (8 s, 24 fps, 192 frames, seamless), made from each still and its data pass
  - a breath of wind through the flowers: a slow travelling sway, stronger near the lens; the stone, the
    crystal and the letter hold perfectly still, and the crystal's light is steady
  - mist drifting through the needles: soft low banks of the scene's own mist colour sliding sideways
  - slow cloud: the sky slides a few pixels over the loop, over a plate filled in behind the needles
  - one grade over stills and loops alike: a gentle curve, blacks lifted toward night blue
  No motes, no rain.
  Wide 1920x1080: H.264 3.6 MB, VP9 0.9 MB. Tall 720x1560: H.264 1.7 MB, VP9 0.4 MB.
  Stills: AVIF and WebP for each (wide 1920 wide, tall 1080 wide); depth maps for each.

The page (index.html)
  Screens narrower than three by four get the tall set (its still, loop, depth and positions); others get the
  wide set. The loop plays over its still; a breathing camera layer (WebGL) draws the loop through its depth,
  pivoted on the stone: a slow drift over about a minute, a few percent of push, a gentle answer to the
  pointer or tilt. Film grain is a small noise tile made in the page. The wordmark and "Software, made on
  purpose." sit small at bottom left.
  The letter: a focusable button over the folded sheet ("Read the note"); it opens with the view easing in
  and the sheet lifting and settling flat (the shared Letter component), lit cool blue from the crystal's
  side. Escape, the close mark or a click outside eases back; reduced motion crossfades. After about seven
  seconds untouched, a soft glint passes over it.
  Sound (muted until the toggle): a faint hush of air through the flowers, a slow wind that rises as the
  pointer moves, and a far low chord (A, E, B) breathing. It goes soft while the note is open.
  Opening: the field rises out of the dark once per session; any touch, key, wheel or click skips it.
  Reduced motion: the still, no opening. Test hooks: ?rest (skip the opening), ?t=0 (still only), ?again.
  Checked with Playwright at 1440x900 and 390x844: no console errors, no overflow, the loop plays, the
  breathing layer runs, the letter opens and closes.

Honest limits
  - The loop's seam is exact in the frames, but the encoders keep fine moving flower texture a touch softer
    by the end of the file than in its first frame, so at the wrap the flowers sharpen very slightly once
    every 8 s (under the page's grain it is hard to see). Higher quality or more keyframes did not cure it.
  - The crystal is deeper than before but still reads a little clean and graphic beside the stone.
  - The needles' surfaces stretch where the scans were tapered; at this distance it reads as weathering.
  - The mist and cloud motion is 2.5D (from the still), so mist does not pass behind one needle and in front
    of another.
